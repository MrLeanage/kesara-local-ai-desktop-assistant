# Photographic hand gestures

Mode: built-in image_gen edit with the photographic Kesara face and original six-pose sheet as identity references. Output: kesara-photo-body-sheet.png. Original generated pixels are retained.

The observed sheet has four columns and three rows. All twelve faces passed local landmark detection. The application aligns the frames, isolates the main person from adjacent-cell fragments, interpolates arm motion with precomputed optical flow, and renders the stable original face with independent speech mouth shapes and blinking. This is a limited photographic gesture cycle, not motion capture or a full 3D human.

Runtime metadata: kesara-photo-body-registration.json and kesara-photo-body-flow.npz. The preparation script computes metadata without changing the source sheet. The portrait prompt is in photo-generation.md.

## Final prompt

Reference image 1 is the REQUIRED photographic fictional Sri Lankan man's face and clothing; reference image 2 supplies his original upper-body proportions and navy jacket/charcoal shirt. Preserve this same identity exactly. Photographic real person appearance with natural skin/hair/clothing texture, NOT a game avatar or 3D render. Output exactly 4 columns x 3 rows of twelve equal portrait-shaped cells, each containing the same waist-up man, centered, whole hair and both hands fully inside every cell with generous margins. Genuine transparent background; no text, logos, dividers, props or accessories. Fixed camera, body scale, face position, torso, lighting and wardrobe across all twelve frames. His face looks directly at viewer, eyes open, lips relaxed closed in every cell. This is ONE smooth restrained conversational open-palms gesture cycle, not twelve unrelated poses. Row-major frames 0 through 11: frame0 relaxed hands low at waist; frame1 hands start rising slightly; frame2 continue gently upward; frame3 hands move a little outward with relaxed curved fingers; frame4 palms begin turning slightly up; frame5 open palms calmly at lower chest, elbows bent; frame6 same gesture near peak, relaxed fingers, palms angled upward/outward (not a wave); frame7 hands begin lowering; frame8 lower more; frame9 near waist; frame10 almost relaxed; frame11 EXACT same rest pose as frame0. Keep shoulders and head stationary. Very small equal changes between successive frames, natural anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand. No exaggerated smiles or gestures. Do not omit hands or crop any fingertips. Use approximately 512x640 portrait cells in a 2048x1920 canvas. Face and torso must remain perfectly registered to support separate lip sync.
